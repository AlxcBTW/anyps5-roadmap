# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This page is a short version; the full text applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their
age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, social and economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect other views and experience, and give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from them.
- Act in the interest of the whole community.

## Not acceptable

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone
represents the project elsewhere. Report a problem to the maintainer,
[@AlxcBTW](https://github.com/AlxcBTW), or through GitHub's "Report content" on the comment
in question. Reports are handled promptly and privately. The maintainer may remove comments
and other contributions, and warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone who breaks these
rules, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
